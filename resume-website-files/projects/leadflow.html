LeadFlow

Multi-Tenant Marketing CRM for Local Service Businesses — Private Beta

Live Demo (sample data)

A Full CRM Platform, Built Solo, Engineered Like a Team Shipped It

LeadFlow is a serverless SaaS platform I architected and built end-to-end: local service businesses capture leads from the web and ad campaigns, move them through visual pipelines, and follow up with automated SMS and email — with per-tenant isolation, analytics, and lead scoring built in. It's in private beta with its first practice in the chiropractic / personal-injury vertical.

What It Does

Lead Capture to Close

Public funnel pages and embeddable forms feed straight into visual pipelines. Every lead carries its full activity history, and a real-time feed streams pipeline events to the dashboard as they happen.

Automation Engine

Trigger-based workflows drive follow-up without human touch: new-lead sequences, stage-change actions, and scheduled campaigns across SMS (Twilio) and email (SES) — with delivery tracking feeding back into analytics.

Campaigns, Scoring & Analytics

Campaign performance, funnel conversion, and pipeline KPIs per tenant, plus behavioral lead scoring so practices call the hottest leads first. Integrations connect scheduling (Jane App) and ad platforms (Meta Marketing API) into the same picture.

Multi-Tenant by Construction

Tenant isolation is enforced at the auth layer (Cognito) and the data layer (tenant-partitioned DynamoDB), not by convention. The data boundary is HIPAA-aware by design: marketing and contact data only, kept strictly separated from anything clinical.

Engineering Rigor

4,543 tests across jest, vitest, and CDK snapshot suites; PR-gated CI; automatic staging deploys; manual, promotable production releases. Infrastructure is code — both environments are reproducible from the repo.

Built AI-First

LeadFlow is the largest proving ground for Sharkrite, my autonomous development engine: 700+ issues have been planned, implemented, reviewed, test-gated, and merged through its pipeline — including a measured 17 autonomous merges in a single day. The two projects evolved together: LeadFlow's scale kept finding Sharkrite's edge cases, and Sharkrite's gates kept LeadFlow's main branch green.

Status & Access

LeadFlow is pre-launch, in private beta with its first practice. The source is in a private repository; the live demo runs on sample data — demo credentials and a code walkthrough are available on request.
